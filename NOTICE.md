# Sources and data attribution

The archived complete graph is a derived MaleCNS v1.0 model, with 166,700
neurons and 25,582,938 anatomical edges. Its normalization, visual projection,
conductance dynamics and engineering learning assumptions are described in
the graph manifest and the model audit. It is not an official physiological
simulation or a demonstrated Snake-playing fruit fly.

MaleCNS data: MaleCNS collaboration / HHMI Janelia FlyEM, University of
Cambridge, MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology, Google Research and upstream
contributors. Data are distributed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

locks are retained in [vendor/stonkfly/THIRD_PARTY.md](vendor/stonkfly/THIRD_PARTY.md)
